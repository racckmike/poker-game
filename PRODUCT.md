# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
A father who imagined this game (handwritten sketch) and the people he plays with: a mixed family group of different ages and comfort levels with technology (children, uncles, friends). Each player joins from whatever they have: phone, laptop, or tablet. None of these devices takes priority.

## Product Purpose
A private online Texas Hold'em table where family and friends play together in real time with virtual chips. Success: Dad creates a room, shares the code, 2–8 people join from their own devices, and they play consecutive hands without help or confusion.

## Positioning
It's a family table, not a casino: no money, no public matchmaking, no accounts. The layout comes from Dad's own sketch, and it is his game.

## Operating Context
Played in real time, usually with everyone connected at once (often on a call or in the same house). Spanish interface. Sessions with many consecutive hands; the 6-second pause between hands is where people talk about what happened.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Texas Hold'em, 2–8 players, a single 52-card deck, virtual chips only.
- Private rooms with code (POKER-XXXXXX), nickname without registration, reconnection by token.
- Authoritative server; the client never computes outcomes.
- Layout fixed by the sketch: "Información" bar on top, Crupier top centre with the deck, five community cards in the centre, players 1–8 clockwise with "Jugador 4 (Yo)" at the bottom centre.
- Undecided: number of decks (Dad mentioned adapting it; one is in use), whether a native app will be built.

## Brand Commitments
- Name in use: "Mesa de Póker". Tagline from the brief: "Tu mesa. Tus amigos. Tu partida."
- Dad's sketch (green felt background, simple layout) is the binding reference for structure.
- The user rejected the first visual pass as looking "too AI": dark green + gold + glow palette, and display serif type with spaced capitals.

## Evidence on Hand
- Sketch: WhatsApp screenshot with the photo of the drawing and Dad's message (on the user's Mac; not in the repo).
- No logos, photos, or real assets. Don't invent testimonials or players.

## Product Principles
1. Dad's sketch rules the structure; the design serves it, it doesn't replace it.
2. Anyone in the family should be able to play without help: obvious actions, legible amounts, nothing hidden.
3. It should feel like a table at home, not a casino or an online betting app.
4. Phone, laptop and tablet are equally important.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Mixed ages: large legible text and numbers, generous touch targets, contrast that holds up in sunlight on a phone, and no reliance on colour alone (suits and states carry text or shape).
